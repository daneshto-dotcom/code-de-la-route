## THE TWO FINDINGS THAT SHAPED EVERYTHING

**1. The 2026 dressing list was already photographed.** `SITE-SURVEY-entrance.md` §6 lists what the
survey saw and then deliberately subtracted: *"tarmac, road signs (N7 / A79 / INRAE / Route de la
Ronde), power lines and poles, cast bollards and chain, the parked cars, the corrugated metal roof,
the wire fencing and concrete blocks."* The 2026 map puts back exactly that, and nothing else.

**2. "Warmer and cooler" was already MEASURED at both ends.** Exactly two palettes were ever
`lifted: true` — `GRASS` and `FOLIAGE`. August 2026 in drought measures straw `#8A7E6E` over
near-black olive `#2B2D23`; 1601 raises that same hue because the estate is tended. So `DROUGHT` and
`FOLIAGE_DRY` are the lift taken off. **The era shift is evidence, not a filter**, and the built
fabric does not change colour at all.

## ARCHITECTURE WORTH KNOWING BEFORE YOU TOUCH ANYTHING

- **ONE GENERATOR, TWO CENTURIES.** `build-zone1.py` emits `zone1/map.json` AND
  `zone1_2026/map.json` from one source. **Never hand-edit either.** A correction applied once
  appears in both. The 1601 map is byte-identical to S109.
- **§11.7 IS A TEST, NOT A PROMISE.** A cell-by-cell assert proves the built fabric is identical
  across eras (96 cells). Any retarget touching a fabric palette must cite the §11.7 row that
  authorises it in `ERA_AUTHORITY` — the gate open/shut, the bollards iron/stone.
- **GLYPH SPACE WAS NEVER FULL.** S109 recorded it as a hard blocker; that was true only of
  printable ASCII. Map rows are JSON strings indexed one CHARACTER at a time, so any BMP character
  is a legal glyph (`Π` pole, `≈` wires, `≡` lane line). Combined with per-map legends, the
  constraint is gone in both directions.
- **OBJECTS DRAW FROM THEIR OWN PALETTE BANK.** Vehicles and NPCs are spawned entities, NOT legend
  glyphs, so their palettes cost the scene nothing. This is the ONLY reason the 2026 map fits in 8
  and the only reason Captain Dael has skin. **Do not put a person or a vehicle in a legend.**
- **`Cinematic` owns the era flip**, not `enterRealm` — so watched, skipped and failed-to-load all
  land in the same place.
