## THE FOUR CORRECTIONS

### P1 — the arrival did not happen

`SaveStore.fresh()` hardcoded the start at **(16,32)**. Captain Dael stands at **(16,31)** and the
fresh facing is `up` — so a brand-new player booted **already touching him and already looking at
him**, and the first key they could press ended the modern world. No walk, no shut gate at a
distance, no traffic at their back.

`zone1_2026/map.json` had declared the right answer all along — `startTile: [14,35]` — and nothing
read it, **because a fresh save was indistinguishable from a resumed one**. `fresh()` always returns
a well-formed `position`, so "is there a saved position" could not be answered by looking at it.

`SaveStore.hasPosition` (`updatedAt > 0`) draws that line. The tile is now the **parcel's** to
declare; the save's job is **which parcel and which century**.

> **Boot now lands at [14,35] — six steps from Dael, not one.**

A second-order tell had been visible for a session: `reach-check` reported
`zone-1-2026: 66/399 reachable from (14,35)` — **the guard had been validating a tile the engine
never used.** They agree now.

### P2 — the road was driving through itself

`build-zone1.py` states it plainly: *"Row 37 is the only FULL-WIDTH run of road on the map."* The
carriageway is **one tile wide**. Direction was a coin flip per spawn, so both directions shared it.

Provable, not arguable: two cars entering opposite ends 1.5 s apart at 120 px/s are both at
x≈298 at t≈2.75 s, on the same row.

Two rules, neither needing the map to change:
1. **One direction at a time** — flow reverses only across an empty road, so head-on overlap is
   *unreachable*, not merely unlikely.
2. **Nothing overtakes** — a follower matches the leader's speed. This is finally what the tractor
   was always for; the file had promised *"a tractor holds everyone up"* while hatchbacks went
   through it at 120 px/s.

**90 simulated seconds of the real code: 0 overlaps · 0 mixed-direction frames · 7 vehicles at peak
· tightest gap 23 px** (against a 24 px headway — car-following biting exactly where it should)
· 1018 vehicle-frames spent queueing.

**Control run** — defeat only the empty-road rule: **712 overlap-frames, 2488 mixed-direction
frames.** The guard is load-bearing, not decorative.

**The engine noise** fired inside `spawn()`, while the vehicle was still entirely off-map and often
seconds away — so the road announced cars as they *left* the world and fell silent as they went by.
It now fires on the camera-centre crossing, latched once per vehicle.

**Four dead speed constants removed.** Unreferenced since S110 moved the fleet into `map.json`,
their only remaining effect was asserting an integral-px/frame invariant that four of the eight
fleet speeds break — and that is **unachievable anyway**: only multiples of 60 px/s qualify at
60 Hz, which allows exactly two speeds, i.e. the conveyor belt the spread exists to avoid. The
header now states what is true.

## A TOOLING FINDING THE NEXT SESSION WILL NEED

**The Browser pane was not compositing, so `requestAnimationFrame` never fired.** `create()` runs
(it is synchronous at scene start) but `update()`, the Clock and the tweens never advance.

**`game.loop.actualFps` reported 60. `game.loop.frame` was 0.** The fps figure is a default, not a
measurement — every "wait for real time" test was meaningless until that was spotted.

To drive the game headlessly, step Phaser yourself — this is what rAF calls, and it advances scene
update, the Clock and the tweens in the engine's own order:

```js
let T = 1000;
const step = (ms) => { const dt = 16.666; for (let e = 0; e < ms; e += dt) { T += dt; game.step(T, dt); } };
```

`scene.time.update()` alone is **not** enough (new events are merged in `preUpdate`), and
`scene.tweens.update()` alone does not advance tweens at all in Phaser 3.90.
