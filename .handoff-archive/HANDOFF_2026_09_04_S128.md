### The three things that mattered most

**The generator would have crashed on the pond's third tile.** `build-atlas.py` sized its
sheet one line *above* its own last registration, so `rows` was computed for `count-1`.
Invisible until the arithmetic crossed a row: 8×22 = 176 slots held 174, and 175 and 176
fitted too. Headroom was exactly **+2**. No document mentioned it; the state-discovery gate
found it. Fixed three ways — the allocation moved, an assert added, and `tile()` **frozen**
once the sheet is sized. The freeze exists because the assertion alone was *proved
insufficient*: one late tile still fits, so nothing fires. Plus a 15th guard reading only
committed artefacts. The atlas is now 179 tiles in an 8×23 sheet — the exact case that
would have crashed.

**A palette "settled and verified" was justified by backwards arithmetic.** S127 moved the
water's shade step off the measured `#686848` because it sat 8.1 CIE76 from `FOLIAGE_DRY` —
the "pond reads as a dry hedge" failure. Re-run against the whole ramp it inverts: the
measured value is 21.95 sRGB / 8.14 CIE76 from its nearest `FOLIAGE_DRY` ink, and the
substitute `#5C7040` is **13.64 / 5.18** — closer to the hedge it was changed to avoid. The
comparison had been made against *one ink*, and only for the value being removed. R8 reads
step 1, identical either way, so no guard ever had jurisdiction. The measurement is restored.

**Every guard passed and the pond rendered a crop field.** Four rendered passes, each one
changing the design, none of it reachable from map data. `Rng` is seeded per tile *name*, so
any motif over ~4 px **stamps**; `Parcel.scatter` at a fixed rate over a rectangle lands its
hits on a **lattice**; and the rim sat at the same value as the `GRAVEL` drive two columns
away. The fourth pass was a 320×288 crop — the player's actual viewport — and it showed the
south shore was still a ruled line.

## RULES THIS SESSION PAID FOR

- **A plant-the-fault run that reports PASS tells you nothing unless the fault you planted is
  the one the guard is for.** I planted "it overflows" for a guard whose job was "someone
  registered late", and only because I planted the wrong one did I learn the guard was aimed
  wrong too.
- **A distance to a neighbour is not a number, it is a minimum over pairs** — and a
  substitution must be measured on the same footing as the thing it replaces.
- **A texture is not a drawing.** Anything you can *name* in a tile will be seen a hundred
  times and become a pattern. Draw grain, not objects. A uniform scatter is not organic.
- **When a gate goes red at close, sort the failures by WHY before deciding what to do with
  any of them.** Ten hard failures, not one false when written.
- **When you arm an alarm, verify the mechanism you are relying on** — and do it while the
  evidence still says failure.
- The shell has **mixed line endings**; git stores LF and `core.autocrlf` expands to CRLF, so
  multi-line anchors written with `\n` do not match on disk. Normalise to LF, patch, write LF.
