The option list is exactly full. offerHandVerbs emits at most four options, and its own comment says four is the ceiling it was specified to. This is why P1 carries a new rules-check.ts as a blocking guard, and why the box arithmetic is fixed in the same phase as the rule table rather than later. The mechanism producing these exceptions is constitutional — §11.0 gives you direct step-by-step authority, and a directed build generates exceptions. The answer is not less direction.
