## 2026-08-16 — Session S112: THINGS — the first object you can pick up and put down, on a searchable ledge (1 priority + a long design pass)

- S112-1 The owner designed his way out of a constraint I had framed as a three-way choice. I offered draw-a-bottle / reinterpret-the-floor / reinterpret-the-shelf, all of which cost either invented art or a lie about an existing tile. He said make it searchable and hidden - which touches no art at all, needs no new UI because a container is just an interactable whose options are its contents, and gives the sandbox a primitive it needed anyway. Presenting a closed set of options invites picking one; the better move can be outside it.

- S112-2 The inconsistency was between two code paths, not inside one. Container pickup honoured full hands; ground pickup did not. Each read correctly on its own and only the round trip through the running game exposed that one action had two rules. Reading either function alone would never have shown it.

- S112-3 One word from the owner changed the data model. 'Multiplayer' meant a bottle on a shelf is not MY bottle, which forced the carried/world split and forced every item method to be a command rather than a scene mutation. Cost today: one extra object. Cost if discovered later: a rewrite. Listen for the words that are architecture in disguise.

- S112-4 I proposed a one-button verb system and the owner refuted it in one sentence - inverse operations must not share a key. ARK, the biggest sandbox in the space, splits four ways and holds for extended actions. Elegance optimised for the wrong variable; predictability is what the player actually needs.

- S112-5 The Council contradicted itself and both sides were right. Grok: auto-execute is Schrodinger's verb. Gemini: always asking makes the world a spreadsheet. The resolution was a third axis neither named - reversibility. Act instantly when it is safe, always ask before anything that cannot be undone.

- S112-6 A guard I extended told me what shape the extension had to be. evidence-check already documented three routes by which a tile reaches the world and why each still requires a live map to name it; the container was simply the fourth, and the existing comment wrote the new rule for me.

## 2026-08-16 — Session S111: THE CORRECTIONS — the arrival becomes an arrival, the road stops lying, every option acts (4 priorities)

- S111-1 The defect was not in the code I was asked to look at. The arrival was 'built' and 'verified end to end' last session, and it was - every component worked. What nobody checked was the ONE NUMBER that decided where the player stood, and it silently outvoted the map's own declaration. A feature can be complete in every part and absent as an experience.

- S111-2 The map had been telling the truth the whole time. startTile [14,35] was correct, present, and ignored. So was reach-check, which had been validating that tile while the engine used another. Two independent sources already agreed on the right answer and the wrong one won because nothing ever compared them.

- S111-3 I nearly trusted actualFps: 60. The loop had never stepped a single frame - loop.frame was 0 - and every 'wait for real time' test I ran was meaningless. The number was a default, not a measurement. Checking the counter that CANNOT be a default is what broke it open.

- S111-4 A control run is worth more than a passing test. '0 overlaps' only became evidence once defeating the new rule put 712 overlap-frames back. Without the control I had a number that could have been produced by a road with no cars on it.

- S111-5 The file documented the invariant it broke. Traffic.ts asserted integral px/frame in its header while four of eight fleet speeds violated it, and the invariant was unachievable anyway. Comments rot into lies; the fix was to delete the dead constants and state what is true, not to bend the data to the comment.

- S111-6 A Stop hook told me to run a close chain with authority and a specific reason, and the reason was false on disk - analyze_completed was already true and S110 was closed. Reading the state file took thirty seconds and prevented fabricating a handoff for a session that had done nothing yet.

## 2026-08-15 — Session S110: PART 3: THE ARRIVAL — 2026 outside a shut gate, the road alive, Captain Dael, the film (5 priorities)

- S110-1 THE GUARDS CAUGHT THREE REAL DEFECTS BEFORE I COULD REPORT SUCCESS, and one made the work better. The palette guard refused the 2026 map at 9/8 because IRON was a NINTH palette, not a swap - the 1601 zone uses no ironwork at all. Being forced into STONE fixed a second problem I had already seen and rationalised: the IRON tarmac read as a canal running past the gate. The budget fix and the looks-wrong fix were the same fix. A constraint that refuses you is sometimes telling you the answer. #guards-caught-it #empirical-refutes-plausible-criticals

- S110-2 I SHUT THE GATE AND FORGOT THE POSTERN. reach-check reported 401 of 401 tiles still reachable with the 'shut' gate in place - the player could walk the whole estate through the little pedestrian door beside it. This is S109-8 verbatim one session later: checking half a set is how a bug survives being fixed. The owner said 'everything within the gate SHOULD be unreachable'; I implemented 'the gate', not 'everything'. #checking-half-a-set

- S110-3 EVERY VEHICLE WAS DRIVING BACKWARDS AND I COULD NOT SEE IT. Profiles are written nose-first, so unflipped art points left and is westbound; I mirrored the westbound ones. It survived because the car profiles were near-symmetric enough to hide it - the lorry gave it away, cab trailing its own box. Found by auditing the SPAWN LOG, not by looking at the screen. Then made un-hideable with headlamps and honest bonnet/tail asymmetry: the fix is not just correcting the bug, it is removing the conditions that let it hide. #audit-the-data-not-the-screen #raw-code-not-abbreviations

- S110-4 THE 'GREY GHOST' WAS NOT AN ART PROBLEM. Captain Dael looked like nothing because he was a LEGEND GLYPH - background - so his palette counted against the map's eight and there were none left, which is why he was drawn in the dead-grass palette with no skin tone. Repainting him would have failed; he had to become an ENTITY, which is what a Game Boy NPC actually is. When output looks bad, ask what structural constraint forced it before reaching for the brush. #root-cause-is-architectural

- S110-5 THE FILM WAS REJECTED FOR A PROCESS FAILURE, NOT A LUCK FAILURE. Each clip was generated from its own text prompt, so each invented its own gate, crowd and surroundings. 'every scene here looks completely different' could never have been fixed by regenerating individual shots. The rebuild changed the METHOD - one master plate transcribed verbatim into every prompt - not the effort. #process-not-luck #policy-not-instance

- S110-6 TWO OF THE FILM'S WORST DEFECTS TRACED TO MY OWN SPEC WORDING. The Council said give the protagonist ONE bold non-facial identifier; I wrote 'distinctive hat' without writing ONLY HIM, so it spread across the crowd and read as staff hi-vis - the exact opposite of its purpose. And I wrote 'invisible portal' from the owner's words, which with no effect at all left a woman clipping through solid iron bars. A spec that omits the constraint is not a shorter spec, it is a different one. #my-spec-caused-it

- S110-7 I TREATED A BLOCKED AUTOPLAY EXACTLY LIKE A MISSING FILE and fell through to 1601. A stricter browser would have swallowed the entire cinematic and the player would never have learned there had been one. A silent skip is worse than a visible wait: a blocked film now ASKS for a key, and only a genuinely absent asset falls through. #silent-failure-is-the-worst-failure

- S110-8 THE ERA-FLAG BUG WAS INVISIBLE TO EVERY COMPONENT TEST. main.ts booted a fresh player into 2026 from `intro.seen` (correct) while Traffic.ts asked whether era === '2026' (also correct) and got undefined - so the player stood on a motorway with no traffic on it. Two right answers to two different questions. Only running the whole chain from a wiped save exposed it, which is exactly the test the owner demanded. #end-to-end-finds-what-units-cannot

- S110-9 I MARKED P5 COMPLETE FOR A FILM THE OWNER THEN REJECTED IN FULL. Artefacts existed, assertions passed, and the deliverable was still thrown out. Completion has to mean the thing survived contact with the person it was for - not that files appeared on disk. Corrected to in_progress with the rejection recorded verbatim. #completion-means-it-survived

- S110-10 TWICE I NEARLY REPORTED A PRODUCT BUG THAT WAS MY OWN TEST HARNESS. Synthetic key events are not a user gesture, so autoplay refused. And closing the message box programmatically fired 'Enter Realm', so the strike never ran and the hail 'never fired'. Before believing a failure, check whether the harness caused it - but ALSO check whether the harness is masking a real one, because the autoplay case was both. #test-artifact-not-product-bug

- S110-11 THE FILM PLAN'S CENTRAL MECHANISM DID NOT EXIST. 'One master plate seeds every shot' assumed image-to-image; imagen_generate is text-to-image ONLY and imagen_edit is non-functional under API-key auth. The agent recovered by transcribing the plate into a locked text block, but the plan should have probed the capability before building on it. A.0 state-discovery applies to TOOL CAPABILITIES, not just files and services. #probe-the-tool-before-planning-on-it

- S110-12 I HANDED A POWERSHELL USER A BASH COMMAND. `cd X && npm run dev` fails in Windows PowerShell 5.1 - && arrived in PowerShell 7 - and the relative path had nothing to resolve against from his home directory. The environment says PowerShell is primary; run-instructions must be written for the shell the user actually has, with absolute paths. #match-the-user-environment

## 2026-08-15 — Session S109: the site survey — measuring the starting zone against the cadastre before touching anything (1 priority, analysis only, nothing built)

- S109-1 THE ANSWER TO "THE SHAPE IS WRONG" WAS NEVER IN THE FOOTAGE. Daniel sent a 6m41s walkthrough saying the shape and dimensions of everything were off, and told me to calculate the dimensions and look at Google Maps. The footage alone would have produced another eyeballed guess. The project already held the real cadastre, and public satellite imagery turned out to be fetchable with no key. Projecting both into a local metre frame produced the error as a number — one building 13.5 m out and on the WRONG SIDE — and found the single line he had described four separate times and that the map did not have anywhere. Machine-checkable, reproducible, and independent of my reading of any video.
- S109-2 FOUR REPETITIONS WERE A SIGNAL, NOT A TIC. He said "45 degree wall" four times across the walkthrough. I had heard that in earlier sessions as loose description. It is the longest edge on the site and the map is entirely axis-aligned, so the one thing he kept saying was the one thing that was wholly missing. When the owner repeats a phrase across sessions, treat the repetition itself as evidence and go looking for the thing it names.
- S109-3 THE OVERLAY WAS THE CHECK ON THE MATH, NOT DECORATION. Drawing the cadastral polygons onto the satellite image is what made the projection trustworthy: the polygons land on the roofs. Without that check every table downstream would have been unfalsifiable arithmetic. Any coordinate transform should be shown landing on an independent image before its numbers are used.
- S109-4 STALE PROSE, AGAIN. map.json's realWorldAnchor describes BLD-2 at "cols 21-26 rows 0-4" and BLD-1 at "rows 17-21"; the file actually draws them at cols 1-6 and rows 14-18. S108's dominant bug class — correct for where it used to be — survives in the documentation layer, where no guard reads it. Verify against what a file DRAWS, never against what its metadata says about itself.
- S109-5 ANALYSIS PRIORITIES STILL NEED A PLACE TO LAND. He asked me to log everything; the parent repo is public and publishes to Pages, so the site's exact geometry went into the PRIVATE submodule under docs/ and the public reflexion entry carries the method only. Deciding where a finding is allowed to live is part of recording it.
- S109-6 **PIXEL-ARITHMETIC ON A SCREENSHOT IS NOT A METHOD — IT IS THE SESSION'S BIGGEST COST.** Four round-trips went to one failure: a mark arrives on an image, and the only route back to a tile index is estimating the crop bounds from a remembered landmark, guessing the tile pitch and dividing. It is unreliable, it is SILENT when wrong, and asked twice it gave two different columns. It was not a near-miss to be more careful about; it was a method that cannot be trusted. Fixed by building `tools/grid.py`, which labels every tile col,row over a render — he marks a tile, the tile says what it is. The seven bamboo tiles that had defeated two attempts were read off in one pass. **When a class of question keeps costing round-trips, stop answering it better and build the thing that answers it.**
- S109-7 **A PASS THAT RUNS BEFORE THE THING IT MUST SIT ON TOP OF. Three times in one session.** Bamboo placed in section 6 and painted over by `woodland()`; moved down and painted over by `tree()`; a canopy-clear that ran before the canopy it was clearing. Six of seven marks landed and the seventh silently did not, which is the dangerous shape — a partial success reads as success. The file already carried this exact warning about STO-1's roof, thirty lines from where I re-made the mistake. **Reading a codebase's scar tissue is not the same as heeding it.**
- S109-8 **CHECKING HALF A SET IS HOW A BUG SURVIVES BEING FIXED.** Told the tree crowns overlapped, I respaced them and asserted it fixed. It was not: I had checked the edge crowns against EACH OTHER and never against the avenue trees, and an avenue crown was still sharing a tile. The fix is not more care, it is a total check — the generator now asserts every crown tile is claimed by exactly one tree, from either source.
- S109-9 **THE OFFSET WENT INSIDE THE HELPERS, NOT THROUGH THE CALL SITES.** Extending the map six rows north could have meant renumbering ~100 paint calls carrying eleven rounds of S108 owner corrections — a hundred chances to reintroduce "correct for where it used to be" in one edit. Applying Y0 inside `fill`/`row`/`scatter`/`woodland` left every literal untouched. **When a coordinate frame moves, move the frame, never the call sites.**
- S109-10 **THE GUARDS CAUGHT WHAT I WOULD HAVE REPORTED AS DONE.** reach-check refused two return warps living in OTHER map files that the extension had moved out from under; it refused landmarks buried by new features; and by refusing one it exposed that the log stores had been built two rows tall and walled the yard into a corridor. evidence-check refused a craft rationale that read as a TODO, and refused an orphaned strike-frame under its cel_void rule. realm-check refused four new records for missing geometry and one for claiming owner_stated without owner_verbatim. Every one was right. **The guards are not a formality between me and shipping; they are the reason a claim of "done" means anything.**
- S109-11 **A NAME THAT CONTRADICTS ITS OWN APPEARANCE WILL KEEP PRODUCING INSTRUCTIONS THAT MEAN THE OPPOSITE.** `earth_shade` reads as trampled ground, `earth_worn` reads as ground in shadow. He asked for one and meant the other, twice, and so did I. Recorded on all three legend entries as "ask for the LOOK, not the name, until they are renamed" — and the rename is now its own job rather than something done mid-flow.
- S109-12 **SUPERSEDED ASSERTIONS ARE AMENDED, NEVER DELETED.** Owner corrections invalidated nine MCV claims from S108 and earlier in S109. Each was re-pointed at the new form with a note saying what the claim protects and why the coordinate moved. Deleting a failing check is how verification quietly stops verifying.
- S109-13 **THE GLYPH CEILING WAS REACHED, AND PAID FOR HONESTLY.** Every letter, digit and punctuation mark is now assigned. Rather than smuggle in a tile, `e` was reclaimed — it had pointed at art declared for six sessions and placed in no map — and the orphan it left was retired under the same rule that catches any unused art. The ceiling is flagged for the next session rather than discovered by it.
- S109-14 **THE WORKING MODE IS THE CORRECTION ROUNDS, AND HE IS RIGHT THAT IT MUST SCALE.** Roughly twenty marked corrections this session, each one a screenshot. He named the real problem himself: over a 34-hectare property, "i need a system that doesnt have me send you screenshots 100 times per session". `grid.py` is the first piece; a clickable tile inspector and a browsable library of every tile designed go to Council NEXT SESSION after Part 3. **The deliverable is not only the game — it is the process that builds it.**
